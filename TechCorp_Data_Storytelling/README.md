# TechCorp | Digital Transformation Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the evolution of TechCorp's digital transformation through operational, customer, financial, technology-adoption and workforce indicators.

The objective is to determine whether the transformation is creating value and identify the main risk that could affect its sustainability.

## Business Question

Is TechCorp's digital transformation generating enough value to justify continued investment, and what should the organization prioritize in the next phase?

## Key Findings

- Productivity increased from 71.65 to 86.79.
- Daily production increased from 447 to 611 units.
- Defect rate decreased from 4.17% to 1.27%.
- Cycle time decreased from 8.52 to 5.30 hours.
- NPS increased from 42 to 66.
- Transformation ROI improved from -15.1% to 10.9%.
- Employee satisfaction decreased from 67 to 55.
- Unfilled technology positions increased from 1 to 20.

## Main Insight

The digital transformation is generating operational, customer and financial value. However, its sustainability is increasingly dependent on the organization's ability to strengthen its workforce and technological capabilities.

## Recommendation

Continue the digital transformation while prioritizing three areas:

1. Strengthen recruitment for critical technology roles.
2. Invest in advanced training and knowledge transfer.
3. Monitor employee satisfaction and workload alongside financial and operational indicators.
